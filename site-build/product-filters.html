Overview

Engineered for Clean, Reliable, Efficient Air

SANPAR compressed air filters are designed to remove dust, liquid, oil aerosols, and vapors from compressed air systems.

Built on a low pressure drop, high dirt-holding design philosophy, these filters ensure:

Energy-efficient operation
Long element life
Consistent air quality

Housed in aluminium die-cast or carbon steel, they are suitable for demanding industrial environments.

Aluminium Die-Cast Lightweight & Corrosion Resistant
Carbon Steel Robust for Heavy Duty Applications

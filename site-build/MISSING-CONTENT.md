## 1. HOMEPAGE — Missing Content

### 1.1 Company History Timeline (MAJOR)
The live site homepage has a **detailed company history timeline** from 1994 to 2025. This is **completely absent** from the new site (home or any other page).

**Missing timeline milestones:**
- **1994 — The Beginning:** SANPAR established Feb 7, 1994. First office inaugurated Feb 22, 1994. First exhibition and purchase order in March 1994. First ECODRAIR dryer sold. Supplied 2000mm diameter air filter to GTRE/DRDO.
- **1995–1997:** Industrial license (1995). First air chiller for defence (1996). Sister concern SANPAR Industries Pvt. Ltd. formed (1997).
- **1998–2000:** First XEROS dryer (1998). Shifted to Whitefield; pressure die-casting dies; ECODRAIR trademark registered (1999). First air chiller to LRDE/DRDO; first industrial dehumidifier; compact brazed aluminium HX for XEROS; first precision AC (2000).
- **2004:** First oil chiller integrated with water chiller. First export to Nageri Sembian, Malaysia.
- **2006–2009:** Purchased industrial property in Bommasandra (2006). Shifted to own facility; received ISO 9001:2015 certification (2009).
- **2010:** PLC-based XEROS dryers. Liquid cooling system for ground testing.
- **2015–2016:** HANNOVER MESSE exhibition, Germany (2015). First airborne cooling system for radar. Airborne LCS qualified/approved by DGAQA and CEMILAC (2016).
- **2017–2020:** AS 9100D certification (2017). First airborne liquid circulation module. Airborne aluminium piping with integrated sensors (2019). Air chillers for LRU cooling; water chillers to CHESS/DRDO (2020).
- **2024:** Bifurcated into SANPAR Industries Pvt. Ltd. and **SANPAR Defence Systems Pvt. Ltd.**
- **2025:** Diesel-cum-grid powered ACM-based air conditioning trolley for defence.

Outcome

Glass moulds free from blowholes. Largest refrigerated dryer by an Indian manufacturer.

Product

Refrigerated Air Dryer (XEROS Series)

Industry

Glass Manufacturing

Requirement

Existing shell and tube dryer had 0.2 bar pressure drop, refrigerant leaks and moisture carry-over causing blowholes.

Solution

XEROS series with aluminium plate heat exchangers (0.1 bar drop) and brazed plate condenser.

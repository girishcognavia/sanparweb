Applications of Air Dryers & Filters in Food & Beverage

Moisture in compressed air threatens product safety, shelf life, and regulatory compliance. SANPAR dryers and filters deliver clean, dry, contaminant-free air that meets food-grade standards — protecting your products and your reputation.

Refrigerated & Desiccant Dryers

Remove moisture from compressed air to prevent bacterial growth, spoilage, and contamination in packaging and processing lines.

Food-Grade Air Filters

Multi-stage filtration ensuring compressed air is free from oil, particulates, and aerosols — meeting food safety regulations.

Process Chillers

Precise temperature control for pasteurisation, fermentation, and cold storage. Consistent quality for beverages and perishables.

Overview

One of the classic product ranges from SANPAR. The Ecodrair series employs tube-in-tube design of air-to-air and evaporator. The dryer incorporates a hot gas bypass valve for modulating capacity during air flow fluctuations.

The digital dew point indicator ensures high accuracy. These compact dryers have a minimal footprint with rear inlet and outlet connections for easy installation. Designed for silent, reliable operation with minimal maintenance.

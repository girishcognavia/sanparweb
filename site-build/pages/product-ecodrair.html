Core Technology

Engineered Components Working Together

Reliable, efficient drying — every component designed for performance and longevity.

Tube-in-Tube Heat Exchanger

Integrated air-to-air exchanger saves >55% power.

Hermetically Sealed Compressor

High efficiency, low noise (<60 dB) and long operational life.

Modulating Capacity Control (Hot Gas Bypass)

Maintains stable dew point under fluctuating loads and prevents freezing.

Capillary Expansion System

Ensures consistent evaporation temperature and dew point.

Centrifugal Moisture Separator

Efficient removal of condensate with zero carryover.

Digital Dew Point Indicator

Real-time monitoring with high accuracy.

Air-Cooled Condenser (Up to 50°C)

High-efficiency aluminium condenser for superior heat rejection.

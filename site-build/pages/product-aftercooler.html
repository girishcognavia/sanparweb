Overview

Compressed air from compressors is hot with large quantities of water vapour. If not removed, it condenses in distribution causing expensive damage to equipment and processes.

Without an aftercooler, a 200 SCFM compressor at 7 bar introduces 170-240 litres of water daily. SANPAR water-cooled aftercoolers are custom made with complete design flexibility.

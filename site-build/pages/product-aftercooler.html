Why Install an After Cooler?

Hot Compressed Air Damages System Efficiency

Compressed air leaving the compressor carries heat and moisture that can affect dryers, pipelines, pneumatic equipment, and production reliability.

SANPAR After Coolers reduce air temperature and remove bulk moisture before it reaches downstream equipment — helping improve system performance and reliability.

Why Proper Cooling Matters

Removes bulk moisture before it enters the system
Protects dryers, filters, valves, and pneumatic equipment
Improves dryer efficiency and overall air treatment performance
Low pressure drop reduces unnecessary energy losses
Helps reduce corrosion, downtime, and maintenance issues
Compact, reliable, and built for continuous industrial operation

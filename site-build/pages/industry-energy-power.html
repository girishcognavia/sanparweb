Application of Our Products in Energy & Power Industry

From fossil fuel plants to renewable energy installations, SANPAR compressed air treatment systems ensure reliable operation of instrumentation, controls, and pneumatic equipment across the energy sector.

Refrigerated Air Dryers

High-capacity Xeros dryers for power plant compressed air systems. Reliable moisture removal for instrument air and control valves.

Desiccant Dryers

Ultra-dry air for switchgear, transformer breathers, and critical substation equipment. Preventing moisture-related insulation failures.

Industrial Chillers

Cooling solutions for generator windings, turbine oil systems, and power electronics. Continuous operation in demanding environments.

Smart Controls

Micro Processor Control Features

Pressure Drop Monitoring

Constantly monitors pressure drop across Plate Heat Exchangers to aid proactive maintenance before choking — ensuring optimum performance.

Condensate Drain Control

Controls the frequency of condensate drain valve opening — configurable on timer or level-based operation.

Power Failure Protection

Safeguards the compressor from frequent start/stop cycles due to incoming power failures.

Delayed Air Entry

Connects to an inlet solenoid to delay compressed air entry, allowing the system to stabilise before drying begins.

Event Log

Records and stores the complete history of dryer health — every tripped event logged for diagnostics and preventive action.

Networked Monitoring

Connect one or multiple units via wired LAN or wireless for centralised monitoring and control. SANPAR also monitors connected units for proactive maintenance.

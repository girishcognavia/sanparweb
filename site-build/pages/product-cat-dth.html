Overview

The DTH series uses four variants of adsorption technology to optimise molecular sieve capabilities for removing moisture from medical air, conforming to BS EN12021:2014 breathing air quality.

Removes moisture to eliminate microbial growth. For ventilators and administering anaesthetics. Achieves -40 deg C pressure dew point and lower.

Overview

SANPAR oil chillers are designed to maintain precise oil temperature in hydraulic systems, grinding operations, and CNC machining centres. Stable oil temperature prevents thermal drift, extends tool life, and ensures consistent part quality.

First developed in 2004 as an integrated oil chiller with water chiller, SANPAR's oil cooling solutions have evolved to serve diverse industrial applications with CFC-free, energy-efficient designs custom-built per application requirements.

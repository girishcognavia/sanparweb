Protecting Process Purity in Corrosive and Volatile Environments

The chemical industry is a vast and complex sector that plays a critical role in the global economy. It involves the production of a wide range of chemicals, including petrochemicals, polymers, and specialty chemicals, which are used in various products and applications across many industries, including automotive, construction, electronics, and pharmaceuticals.

Dry compressed air is commonly used in the chemical industry for various applications where moisture can have a negative impact on the chemical processes and change its properties.

Shelf Life Management & Product Applications in Chemical Industry

Shelf life of a chemical refers to the period of time during which the chemical remains stable and effective when stored under appropriate conditions. It is the time from the date of manufacture until the product becomes unsuitable for its intended use due to changes in its physical or chemical properties.

The management of chemical shelf life involves ensuring that chemicals are stored under the appropriate conditions and that their usage is within the recommended timeframe. This includes proper labelling and documentation of chemical inventory, appropriate storage conditions (such as temperature and humidity), regular inspection and testing of chemicals, and appropriate disposal of expired chemicals.

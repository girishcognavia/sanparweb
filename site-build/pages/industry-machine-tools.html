Application of Chillers & Compressed Air Dryers in Machine Tool Industry

A compressed air dryer is a device used to remove moisture and other contaminants from compressed air systems. When air is compressed, the temperature increases, causing water vapor to condense and collect in the compressed air system. This moisture can cause corrosion, reduce the lifespan of pneumatic equipment, and compromise the quality of the compressed air supply. A compressed air dryer works by lowering the dew point of the compressed air, which causes the moisture to condense and be removed from the system.

Dry compressed air is used in the operation of machine tools to power pneumatic equipment, such as valves, actuators, Automatic tool changer (ATC), Pneumatic doors, Pneumatic clamping and declamping and in motors. However, compressed air can contain contaminants such as water vapor, oil, and particulates, which can damage the pneumatic equipment and negatively impact the quality of the finished products.

Chillers for the machine tool industry are cooling systems used to regulate the temperature of cutting tools and workpieces during the machining process. Machine tools generate a lot of heat due to the high speed and friction generated during the machining process. This heat can cause thermal expansion and distortion of the workpiece, which can lead to inaccuracies and poor quality finished products. Additionally, high temperatures can also damage the cutting tool, leading to premature wear and breakage.

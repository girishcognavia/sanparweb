Application of Chillers & Compressed Air Dryers in Machine Tool Industry

Machine tools demand precise temperature control and contaminant-free compressed air. SANPAR delivers both — protecting your equipment, improving accuracy, and extending tool life.

Coolant Chillers

Regulate cutting and grinding fluid temperatures to prevent thermal expansion, workpiece distortion, and premature tool wear on CNC machines.

Compressed Air Dryers

Clean dry air for pneumatic valves, actuators, ATC systems, and clamping mechanisms. Prevents corrosion and equipment damage.

Oil Chillers

Precision hydraulic oil temperature control for spindle bearings, hydraulic systems, and EDM equipment. Consistent accuracy shift after shift.

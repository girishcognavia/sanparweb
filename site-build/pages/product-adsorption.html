Overview

SANPAR has expertise and proven track record to design dryers supplying clean dry air using adsorption techniques. Twin-tower reversible process - moisture is adsorbed during drying and desorbed during regeneration.

The heart consists of desiccants (molecular sieves, activated alumina, silica gel), pressure vessels, directional valves, and control logic. Low pressure drop and low purge loss is the hallmark of SANPAR dryers.

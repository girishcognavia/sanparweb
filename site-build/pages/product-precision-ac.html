Overview

The SANPAR Precision Air Conditioner precisely controls temperature and humidity with advanced components working in seamless harmony.

Maintains tolerance of plus/minus 1 deg C in temperature and plus/minus 2% in relative humidity. For server rooms, labs, and sensitive electronics requiring 20-25 deg C at 40-55% RH year-round.

Smart Controls

Integration of Control Mechanisms

Sophisticated electronic microprocessor with control logic developed by SANPAR. Sensors for refrigerant compressors, fans, pumps, and control valves enable precise climate management with multi-unit tandem operation capability.

EC Variable Speed Fans

Electronically Commutated variable speed fans deliver significant power savings and low-noise operation.

Hot Gas Re-Heat Dehumidification

Energy-efficient dehumidification by bypassing energy normally rejected to the condenser — eliminating the need for external reheat.

Power Failure Protection

Safeguards equipment from frequent start/stop cycles due to incoming power failures.

Event Log

Detailed history of every system event — tripped events, temperature excursions, and maintenance alerts all logged.

Networked Monitoring

Multi-unit tandem monitoring and control via wired LAN or wireless connectivity for centralised climate management.

Condensate Drain Control

Automated condensate drain management — configurable on timer or level-based operation for consistent performance.

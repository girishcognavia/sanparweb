Overview

Compact electronic controls, drives, servos and PLCs are extremely sensitive to heat and contamination. Smaller cabinets make temperature control difficult.

The Xerion series works on the condensation principle. A fan draws humid air through an evaporator coil, cooling below dew point. Fully automatic with built-in hygrostat.

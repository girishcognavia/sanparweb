Faster Cycles. Fewer Rejects. Lower Energy Costs.

Plastics is a major contributor to many aspects of modern life, from consumer products to industrial applications, It is a diverse and rapidly evolving sector that produces a wide range of products made from synthetic or semi-synthetic materials.

SANPAR have increased interest in providing customised thermal solutions for controlling the temperature and viscosity of plastic materials during various stages of processing.

As the demand for compressed air in the plastic industry is expected to continue to grow as the industry strives our products will increase productivity, reduce costs, and improve product quality.

Application of Dry Compressed Air & Cooling Systems in Plastics

Both commodity and engineering plastics processing relies on dry compressed air and precise temperature control. SANPAR products deliver energy efficiency, quality, safety, and cost-effectiveness across every moulding method.

Injection Moulding Chillers

Precise mould temperature control for faster cycle times, fewer rejects, and consistent part quality across high-volume production runs.

Compressed Air Dryers

Dry air for blow moulding, pneumatic conveying of resin pellets, and driving actuators. Prevents moisture contamination in finished products.

Extrusion & Thermoforming Cooling

Temperature-controlled cooling for extrusion lines and thermoforming stations. Consistent material properties and dimensional accuracy.

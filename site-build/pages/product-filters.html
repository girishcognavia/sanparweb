Overview

SANPAR is one of the leading organisations manufacturing microfilters for critical applications including painting, instrumentation, pneumatics, and textiles.

The design philosophy is low flow, long life rather than high flow, short life - in accordance with energy conservation in compressed air treatment. Cylindrical elements housed in aluminium die-cast or carbon steel housings.

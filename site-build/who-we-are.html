About SANPAR

Built by Engineers. Proven by Industry.

SANPAR was founded in 1994 with a single product and a clear ambition: to manufacture compressed air treatment systems that match the quality of the world's best. Today, with 14 product lines, 200+ engineers, and an AS 9100D-certified manufacturing facility in Bengaluru, we serve customers across aerospace, defence, pharmaceutical, steel, and nine other industry verticals.

We own the entire value chain — from R&D and thermal simulation through manufacturing, testing, installation, and lifetime support. Every system that leaves our facility has been designed, assembled, and validated by our team. That's not outsourcing with a label. That's engineering with accountability.

Our Journey

Three Decades of Engineering Excellence

1994

The Beginning

SANPAR officially established on February 7, 1994. First office inaugurated February 22. First industrial exhibition and first purchase order in March 1994. Sold the first compressed air dryer branded as ECODRAIR. Supplied a 2000mm diameter air filter to GTRE / DRDO.

1995–1997

Building Foundations

1995: Industrial license for manufacturing. 1996: First air chiller for defence application. 1997: Formed sister concern SANPAR Industries Pvt. Ltd.

1998–2000

Product Innovation Era

1998: First XEROS compressed air dryer. 1999: Shifted to Whitefield; developed pressure die-casting dies; ECODRAIR trademark registered. 2000: First air chiller to LRDE / DRDO; first industrial dehumidifier; compact brazed aluminium heat exchangers for XEROS dryers; first precision air conditioner.

2004

Global Footprint Begins

First oil chiller integrated with water chiller. First export order to Nageri Sembian, Malaysia — marking SANPAR's entry into international markets.

2006–2009

Infrastructure Expansion

2006: Purchased industrial property in Bommasandra. 2009: Shifted to own facility at Bommasandra Industrial Estate. Received ISO 9001:2015 certification.

2010

Advancing Technology

PLC-based XEROS air dryers introduced. Liquid cooling system developed for ground testing applications.

2015–2016

International Recognition & Defence Innovations

2015: Exhibited at HANNOVER MESSE, Germany — the world's largest industrial technology fair. First airborne cooling system for airborne radar. 2016: Airborne Liquid Cooling System (LCS) qualified and approved by DGAQA and CEMILAC.

2017–2020

Aerospace & Defence Advancements

2017: AS 9100D (EN 9100:2018) certification; first airborne liquid circulation module. 2019: Airborne aluminium piping system with integrated sensors. 2020: Air chillers for LRU cooling; water chillers to CHESS / DRDO.

2024

Organisational Growth

Bifurcated into two entities: SANPAR Industries Pvt. Ltd. and SANPAR Defence Systems Pvt. Ltd. — reflecting the growing scale and specialisation of our defence portfolio.

2025

Cutting-Edge Development

Diesel-cum-grid powered ACM-based air conditioning trolley for defence — pushing the boundaries of mobile climate control for field applications.

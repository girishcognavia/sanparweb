What We Offer

Sustained Performance Support

Installation & Commissioning

Equipment is handed over only after successful commissioning. SANPAR believes that commissioning is complete only when the end user is proficient in the maintenance of the equipment.

Preventive Maintenance

Regular maintenance checks help identify problems early and prevent costly downtime. Service engineers periodically check your systems and optimise them for energy efficiency. A service warranty is maintained with service reports to track individual system lifeline.

Annual Service Contracts

ASC with a Service Engineer visiting bi-monthly or quarterly during the contract year. Fixed service prices avoid financial surprises. The total responsibility plan provides ultimate risk coverage with eligibility for upgrades and original spare replacements.

Spare Parts Supply

Most spares dispatched within 48 hours, subject to supplier availability. Acquiring original, certified and compliant spares maintains the lifetime of equipment and overall system efficiency. Only genuine parts can guarantee performance and integrity.

Troubleshooting

Trained engineers with comprehensive knowledge of Compressed Air Technology and Refrigeration Concepts deliver excellent troubleshooting. In our experience, most issues are solved by giving tips via telephone — reinforcing our belief in our products and solutions.

Emergency Response

Top priority emergency response to SOS calls for assistance from your nearest service centre. Our constantly upgraded service network ensures minimised response times to keep your production schedules on track.

Need Support?

Whether you need scheduled maintenance, emergency assistance, or spare parts — our service team is ready.

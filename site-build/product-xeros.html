How?

How It Works

Xeros uses a proven refrigeration cycle to cool the compressed air, condense the moisture and deliver consistent dry air.

1. Moist Air In

Hot, humid air enters the dryer.

→

2. Air Cooling

Refrigeration cools the air.

→

3. Moisture Condenses

Water vapor turns into liquid.

→

4. Water Removal

Moisture is removed via automatic drain.

→

5. Dry Air Out

Clean, dry air exits the dryer.

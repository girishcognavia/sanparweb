Why Air Chillers?

Engineered for Performance

Stable air temperature is critical for process consistency, product quality, and equipment longevity.

  • Process consistency
  • Tool life extension
  • Energy savings
  • Reduced downtime
  • Constant output quality
  • Lower running costs

Ideal For

Industries that rely on constant-temperature compressed air for critical processes.

  • Compressed Air

  • Chemical
Smart Controls

Pressure Drop Monitoring

Constantly monitors pressure drop across Plate Heat Exchangers to aid proactive maintenance before choking.

Condensate Drain Control

Controls the frequency of condensate drain valve opening — timer or level-based operation.

Power Failure Protection

Safeguards the compressor from frequent start/stop cycles during incoming power failures.

Delayed Air Entry

Inlet solenoid delays compressed air entry, allowing the system to stabilise before drying begins.

Event Log

Records complete dryer health history — every tripped event logged for diagnostics and preventive action.

Networked Monitoring

Connect one or multiple units via wired LAN or wireless for centralised monitoring and control.

Overview

Reliable condensate removal for every system

Condensate is an unavoidable byproduct of compressed air systems. If not removed effectively, it can lead to corrosion, equipment damage, and reduced efficiency.

SANPAR drain valves automatically remove condensate from filters, dryers, receivers, and pipelines — ensuring clean, dry air and uninterrupted operation.

With multiple drain types available, you can select the right solution based on application, budget, and performance needs.

Why It Matters

Prevents Equipment Damage

Avoids corrosion and premature failures.

Maintains Air Quality

Stops water carryover downstream.

Improves Efficiency

Eliminates pressure drops and air loss.

Reduces Maintenance

Keeps systems clean and reliable.

Types of Drains

Float Type (Zero Air Loss)

Mechanical operation based on condensate level.

No air loss, no electrical supply, highly reliable.

Timer Controlled (Solenoid)

Discharges condensate at set intervals.

Simple and cost-effective solution.

Level Sensor Controlled

Discharges only when condensate is present.

Optimized performance with zero air loss.

Mechanical Drains

Robust and simple design.

Ideal for basic applications.

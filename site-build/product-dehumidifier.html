Why Humidity Control?

Prevent Moisture Problems

Protect equipment, materials, and processes from:

  • Condensation

  • Product quality issues

Ideal For

  • Clean Rooms
  • Libraries
  • Storage Areas
How?

Refrigeration-Based Dehumidification

Humid air passes over a low-temperature evaporator coil. Moisture condenses into water and is drained out, while dry air is recirculated back into the room.

Excellence Since 1994

We are Sanpar

Established in 1994, SANPAR has over three decades of experience in application, manufacturing, engineering and R&D in the field of airing solution.

We have proved ourself strong in each step in the chain of technology, Design, Development, Manufacturing and Delivery.

Our Manufacturing facility is located in Banglore, India is manned by our team of qualified, skilled employees who work seamlessly to produce world class products.

From inventions to innovations, energy responsibility to environment stewardship, SANPAR provides airing solutions to complex thermal engineering applications.

we have constantly challenged the norms and consistently pushed the boundaries to deliver solutions that earned us a reputation of trust and quality.

SANPAR vests its energy to deliver innovative solutions for the treatment of compressed air. Every assignment helps leverage our expertise while helping us implement turnkey projects with customized precision. The valuable experience earned through varying projects makes us industry ready when it comes to customization and special requirement commissioning.

The technical innovation in the design and manufacture of Compressed Air Treatment Systems, Industrial Cooling System and Advanced Cooling Systems have changed the industry perception, armed with impeccable product quality, and customer support has helped us claim a commendable position when conservation of energy and environment is paramount. The different clientele requires special attention, our service and application-based marketing network spread across the globe ensure just that when it comes to providing solution that not only deliver but also ensure an enduring and profitable relationship.

Our Journey of Innovation

1994 – The Beginning

  • SANPAR was officially established on February 7, 1994, marking the beginning of its journey in industrial air and thermal engineering.
  • The company’s first office was inaugurated on February 22, 1994, laying the foundation for its operations.
  • In March 1994, SANPAR participated in its first industrial exhibition and successfully received its first purchase order, initiating its business
    operations.
  • Later that year, SANPAR sold its first compressed air dryer, branded as ECODRAIR, demonstrating its product innovation capabilities.
  • SANPAR also supplied a 2000 mm diameter air filter to GTRE / DRDO, indicating its early contribution to the Indian defence sector.

Our Journey of Innovation

1995 – 1997 – Building Foundations

  • In 1995, SANPAR obtained its industrial license for manufacturing, formalizing its position as a certified manufacturer in the industry.
  • By 1996, the company achieved a significant milestone by delivering its first air chiller for a defence application, marking the beginning of its long
    standing association with defence technology.
  • In 1997, SANPAR expanded its operational structure by forming its sister concern, known as SANPAR Industries Pvt. Ltd.

Our Journey of Innovation

1998 – 2000 – Product Innovation Era

  • In 1998, SANPAR developed and successfully sold its first XEROS compressed air dryer, introducing a new product line to its portfolio.
  • During 1999, SANPAR shifted its operations to Whitefield, where it developed pressure die-casting dies for use in filters and drain assemblies.
  • That same year, SANPAR also secured trademark registration for its ECODRAIR brand, establishing its intellectual property rights.
  • The year 2000 witnessed multiple technological innovations including the supply of SANPAR’s first air chiller to LRDE / DRDO, the development of its first industrial dehumidifier, and the introduction of compact brazed aluminum heat exchangers for use in XEROS dryers.
  • SANPAR also developed its first precision air conditioner during this time, further diversifying its product offerings.

Our Journey of Innovation

2004 – Global Footprint Begins

  • In 2004, SANPAR delivered its first oil chiller integrated with a water chiller, showing advancement in combination cooling systems.
  • That same year, SANPAR executed its first export order to Nageri Sembian in Malaysia, marking the start of its international business footprint.

Our Journey of Innovation

2006 – 2009 – Infrastructure Expansion

  • SANPAR purchased industrial property in Bommasandra in 2006, paving the way for its future expansion.
  • By 2009, the company had shifted its operations to its own facility at the Bommasandra Industrial Estate, creating a stronger infrastructure foundation.
  • In the same year, SANPAR received ISO 9001:2015 certification, reaffirming its commitment to international quality standards.

Our Journey of Innovation

2010 – Advancing Technology

  • In 2010, SANPAR introduced its PLC-based XEROS air dryers, integrating programmable control technology for enhanced performance.
  • That year, the company also executed a liquid cooling system for ground testing applications, showcasing its innovation in engineered thermal systems.

Our Journey of Innovation

2015 – 2016 – International Recognition & Defence Innovations

  • In 2015, SANPAR participated in the HANNOVER MESSE exhibition in Germany, one of the world’s most prestigious industrial trade fairs, marking its global visibility.
  • That year, SANPAR also executed its first airborne cooling system specifically designed for airborne radar applications.
  • In 2016, SANPAR’s airborne Liquid Cooling System (LCS) was fully qualified and approved by DGAQA and CEMILAC, solidifying the company’s presence in aerospace and defence sectors.

Our Journey of Innovation

2017 – 2020 – Aerospace & Defence Advancements

  • In 2017, SANPAR achieved certification for the Aerospace Standard EN 9100:2018 (AS 9100 D) and supplied its first airborne liquid circulation module.
  • In 2019, SANPAR further advanced its capabilities by supplying an airborne aluminum piping system equipped with integrated sensors, enhancing its product performance in critical applications.
  • In 2020, the company continued its strong association with the defence sector by supplying air chillers for LRU (Line Replaceable Unit) cooling and water chillers to CHESS / DRDO.

Our Journey of Innovation

2024 – Organizational Growth

  • In 2024, SANPAR strategically bifurcated into two specialized entities: SANPAR Industries Pvt. Ltd. and SANPAR Defence Systems Pvt. Ltd., enabling a focused approach toward both industrial and defence markets.

Our Journey of Innovation

2025 – Cutting-Edge Development

  • In 2025, SANPAR developed a diesel-cum-grid powered ACM-based air conditioning trolley for use in defence applications, showcasing its continued leadership in engineering customized and mission-critical cooling systems.
